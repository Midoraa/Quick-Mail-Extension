100% Client-Side Extension

Privacy Policy — Quick Mail: Mini Inbox for Gmail

Effective Date: November 21, 2026 • Compatible with Google Chrome & Microsoft Edge

Quick Mail: Mini Inbox for Gmail ("Quick Mail") is a 100% client-side browser extension built with a single purpose: helping you monitor unread Gmail counts and quickly read, organize, and reply to emails directly from your browser Popup or Side Panel.

1. Data Accessed via Gmail API (gmail.modify)

When you choose to sign in with your Google account, Quick Mail requests only the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.modify OAuth 2.0 scope to access:

  • Email profile & label counts: Your Gmail address and unread/total counts for labels such as Inbox, Starred, Drafts, and Spam.
  • Email metadata & message bodies: Sender, recipient, subject, snippet, date, and message contents for messages in Inbox, Starred, Sent, Drafts, Spam, and Trash so you can read them securely inside the extension.
  • User-initiated message actions: Marking read/unread, starring/unstarring, archiving, moving to Trash, recovering from Spam/Trash, creating/updating/sending/discarding drafts, and sending replies explicitly submitted by you. Quick Mail never requests broad mail.google.com permissions and never permanently deletes messages.

2. Purpose of Use

All data accessed from Google is used exclusively to provide Quick Mail's user-facing mini-inbox features inside your browser:

  • Updating the toolbar badge unread counter (Inbox unread only) and optional desktop notifications via chrome.alarms and chrome.notifications.
  • Displaying folders, reading emails safely after strict XSS sanitization, and sending replies or drafts triggered by your actions.

3. Zero Backend & Local-Only Storage

Quick Mail operates with zero developer backend servers, zero telemetry, zero analytics, and zero third-party data sharing. All requests communicate directly between your browser and official Google endpoints:

  • https://gmail.googleapis.com/gmail/v1/users/me/* — direct Gmail REST API v1 operations.
  • https://accounts.google.com/o/oauth2/v2/auth & https://oauth2.googleapis.com/revoke — authenticating and revoking OAuth 2.0 tokens via chrome.identity.
  • chrome.storage.local: Stores local preferences (polling interval, notification toggle, optional Edge Web Client ID, last opened folder) and short-lived Edge Web Flow OAuth tokens with expiration timestamps.
  • chrome.storage.session: Temporarily stores unsent reply and draft edits solely in browser session memory.
  • Privacy-preserving rendering: Plain text is preferred, HTML bodies are strictly sanitized, and remote images are hidden by default to block tracking pixels.

4. Google API Services User Data Policy (Limited Use)

Quick Mail: Mini Inbox for Gmail's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • Quick Mail does not sell, rent, or transfer your Google user data to advertisers, data brokers, or third parties.
  • Quick Mail does not use Gmail data to train generalized AI/ML models.
  • Human access to your email data is technically impossible because no external servers receive or store your messages.

5. Revoking Access & Deleting Local Data

You stay in full control of your data and can revoke access at any time:

  • Click "Sign out" inside Quick Mail's Settings view to immediately revoke the active OAuth token with Google and wipe all cached tokens, sync state, and session drafts from your browser.
  • Or remove access directly from your Google Account Permissions page.
